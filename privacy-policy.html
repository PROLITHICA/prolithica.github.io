Privacy policy

Updated 14 September 2026 · Website enquiries

This policy explains the information handled by the Prolithica Technologies website and its project enquiry form. It does not describe the practices of separately operated client systems.

Information you provide

The project enquiry form asks for your name, work email, organisation, area of interest, and a brief project description. Timeline and budget are optional. Please do not submit confidential documents, account credentials, financial records, or operational data.

Applications

The application page asks for your name, email, area of interest, background and skills, an optional portfolio or CV link, and motivation. Answers remain on the page as you move between steps and are sent through FormSubmit to info@prolithica.com only after you select Send Application. We use them to review your expression of interest and communicate with you. Please share only information you are comfortable sending by email. Applications do not create a commitment to employment or participation.

How enquiries are handled

When you submit the form, the information is sent to FormSubmit, a third-party form service, for delivery to info@prolithica.com. Prolithica uses your enquiry to understand your request and communicate with you about it. FormSubmit and email providers process information under their own policies. See FormSubmit’s privacy policy.

The form displays success only after the service confirms acceptance. This confirms acceptance by the service, not that an email has been read. If submission fails, the page retains your entries so you can retry or contact us by email.

Website hosting and browser storage

This website does not add analytics trackers, advertising cookies, or browser storage for form entries. Enquiry fields remain on the page while it is open. The hosting provider may process technical request information such as IP addresses and browser details to serve the website. Third-party services may have their own technical logging or anti-spam processing.

Confidentiality and research

We do not publicly disclose client identities, systems, internal workflows, architecture, screenshots, or operational data. Public examples on this website are illustrative and use synthetic data. Our public research partnership is separate from confidential client delivery and does not imply that client information is shared with research partners.

Retention and requests

To ask about information you have submitted, request a correction or deletion, or discuss how long an enquiry is held, contact info@prolithica.com. Any applicable retention requirements can be discussed in that response.

External links and email

Links to Aphorion Labs, GitHub, the sign-in service, and other external destinations are governed by those services’ policies. Email links open your chosen email application. Information you send by email is handled by the relevant email services.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the website’s information handling changes.

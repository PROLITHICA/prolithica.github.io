Website terms

Updated 14 September 2026

This website introduces Prolithica Technologies and provides a way to enquire about potential work.

Capabilities and examples

Solution descriptions explain capabilities and potential project scope. They do not imply that every module is an existing product or a completed deployment. Demonstration interfaces and diagrams are illustrative and use synthetic information.

Project discussions

Submitting an enquiry does not establish a project agreement. Scope, responsibilities, costs, timelines, support, and any confidentiality arrangements are agreed separately for an engagement.

Research partnership

Aphorion Labs is Prolithica’s research partner. Aphorion’s research and inventions are attributed to Aphorion Labs. Exploratory research is not presented as a deployed client solution.

Responsible use

Use the enquiry form for legitimate requests and share only information you are authorised to provide. Start with a high-level overview; confidential requirements can be discussed through an agreed private channel.

External websites

External links lead to separately operated services. Their terms and privacy policies apply when you use them.

Contact

For questions about this website, email info@prolithica.com. Our privacy policy explains how website enquiries are handled.
